### The mark

`src/assets/logo.svg` — a solid `#40BA21` square with a white knock-out of a chip die with pins.
It is a **reversed** mark: the green field _is_ the logo. Consequences that are easy to get wrong:

- there is no dark-mode variant and no `prefers-color-scheme` block in `favicon.svg`;
- it is never recoloured to `currentColor`;
- `<meta name="theme-color">` is deliberately **not** the green.

`pnpm icons` regenerates the whole raster set in `public/` from this one file.

## 2. Domains

| Domain                                       | Role                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `www.innochipdesign.ru`                      | 🟡 proposed canonical — public, memorable, matches the brand |
| `innochipdesign.campus.innopolis.university` | 🟡 proposed alias — should 301 to the canonical              |

🔴 **Blocking the first production build.** `SITE_URL` bakes one origin into every canonical, sitemap
entry and OG image URL. Serving the same HTML on both hosts without a redirect produces duplicate
content and splits any search ranking between them.

Recommended resolution: canonical `https://www.innochipdesign.ru`, with the campus host issuing a
permanent redirect at the university's reverse proxy. If the university host must serve content
directly, build the image twice with different `SITE_URL` values rather than serving one build on
two names.

### URL layout — portal at `/`, club at `/club`

One Astro build, served at the domain root. No `base` config; `/club` is a real directory.

```
/                 portal — the switchboard
/club             club home
/club/about
/club/join
/club/contact
```

**The portal is a switchboard, not a page.** Its whole job is to get a visitor to the right property
in one click: logo, name, three tiles. If it ever grows a projects section or an about paragraph it
has become a homepage and the `/club` split has lost its point.

Not built yet, and the reason the split exists: `/club/projects`, `/club/events`,
`/club/resources`, `/club/equipment`.

## 5. Meetings and joining

| Field            | Value                                                            |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meeting time     | **Every Saturday, 12:00**                                        |
| Season           | Spring and fall semesters only                                   |
| Location         | **Announced per meeting** in the chat — never a fixed room       |
| Selection        | **None. Everyone is welcome.**                                   |
| Application form | <https://engineer.yadro.com/chip-design-school/#applicationForm> |

Two consequences that shape the pages:

**1. No dated "next meeting" banner.** Asked who would keep it current, the leader answered
"nobody". What ships instead is a _standing recurring sentence_ — "We meet every Saturday at 12:00
during the spring and fall semesters; the room is announced in the chat" — which stays true for
years without maintenance and points at the chat as the live source. A stale date makes a site look
abandoned; a standing sentence does not.

**2. The application form is YADRO's, not the club's.** `engineer.yadro.com/chip-design-school` is
the School of Digital Circuit Synthesis, a free two-semester programme run by YADRO with MIET, MFTI
and ITMO teaching staff. The club is one of its clusters.

So `/club/join`'s first step sends people to **a third party's programme application**, not to a club
membership form. The page must say that plainly, or a visitor who wanted to join the club on Saturday
is confused by an unrelated school signup. The shipped framing:

> 1. **Sign up for the Chip Design School** — the free YADRO/MIET course our sessions follow. ↗
> 2. **Join the Telegram chat** — where the room and the week's topic are posted. ↗
> 3. **Just come on Saturday.** No application is required to show up.

Step 3 is deliberate and should survive editing: a form as the only path filters out exactly the shy
first-years a student club most wants to reach.

🟡 This framing was drafted, not supplied. Confirm it, or supply a club-specific form.

## 6. Partners and competitions

### Partner — ADV-T

**ООО «Адв-Тех»**, <https://adv-t.ru> — a full-cycle systems integrator based in Moscow. Equipment
and software supply, IT infrastructure, deployment, audit and consulting. ~20 years in IT services.

🔴 **Unconfirmed before this is printed:** the _relationship_ (sponsor / partner / employer of
members) and _how they want to be credited_ — "ADV-T", the Russian legal name, or a supplied logo. A
wrongly-worded credit for a company is worse than no credit.

The leader also asked for a "Your company could be here" card: a dashed-outline `mailto:` card at the
end of the partners row, shown only alongside at least one real partner. Alone it would read as
"nobody works with us".

### Competition — YADRO SoC Design Challenge

<https://edu.yadro.com/soc-design-challenge/> — an engineering hackathon run by YADRO and MIET at
Zelenograd, focused on the final stages of a RISC-V SoC project before manufacturing. In person,
teams of 2–3 full-time students. Tracks: Topological Design · RTL Design · UVM Verification · System
Verification · DFT Structures. The 2024 edition drew 260+ participants from 16 cities.

Club record as supplied: participated 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026; won prizes in 2024.

🔴 **The 2024 result needs three details before it can appear as an award: which track, which
placement, and the team members' names.** The club's placement could not be verified from public
sources, and an unverifiable "we won prizes" line is exactly what a visiting recruiter checks. Until
they are supplied, the site says only the checkable version, and **must not** be upgraded to "won":

> The club has fielded teams at the YADRO SoC Design Challenge every year since 2023 and placed in 2024.
