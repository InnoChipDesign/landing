## 2. Domains

Two domains were named:

| Domain | Role |
|---|---|
| `www.innochipdesign.ru` | 🟡 **proposed canonical** — public, memorable, matches the brand |
| `innochipdesign.campus.innopolis.university` | 🟡 proposed alias — 301-redirects to the canonical |

🔴 **Blocker: which one is canonical must be decided before the first production build.** `SITE_URL`
is a build argument (`08-deployment.md` §2); canonical URLs, the sitemap, OG image URLs and JSON-LD
all bake it in. Serving the same HTML on both hosts without a redirect produces duplicate content and
splits any search ranking between them.

Recommended resolution: canonical `https://www.innochipdesign.ru`, and the campus host issues a
permanent redirect at the university's reverse proxy. If the university host must also serve content
directly, build the image twice with different `SITE_URL` values rather than serving one build on two
names.

### URL layout — portal at `/`, club at `/club`

Confirmed 2026-08-07. **One Astro build, served at the domain root. No `base` config.**

```
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/                    portal — grid of club web properties
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club                club home
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/projects       project directory
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/projects/<slug>
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/about
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/events
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/resources
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/join
https://www.innochipdesign.ru/club/contact
```

Why **not** `base: '/club'`: with `base`, Astro would mount the whole site under the prefix and `/`
would be unbuildable from this repo. We need to own both, so the prefix is expressed as a directory
in `src/pages/club/` instead. Every internal link is written relative to the site root and there is
no `import.meta.env.BASE_URL` juggling anywhere.

## 5. Meetings, joining and membership

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Meeting day | **Every Saturday, 12:00** |
| Meeting season | Spring and fall semesters only |
| Location | **Announced per meeting** — never a fixed room |
| Selection | **None. Everyone is welcome.** |
| Application form | https://engineer.yadro.com/chip-design-school/#applicationForm |
| Questions / chat | https://t.me/innochipdesign |

Two consequences that shape the pages:

1. **No dated "next meeting" banner.** The leader answered "nobody" to who would keep it current, so
   it is dropped (see `05-sitemap-and-pages.md` §7). What ships instead is a *static recurring*
   sentence — "We meet every Saturday at 12:00 during the spring and fall semesters; the room is
   announced in the chat" — which is true for years without maintenance, and points at the chat as the
   live source. This is the correct trade: a stale date makes a site look abandoned; a standing
   sentence does not.

2. **The application form is YADRO's, not the club's.** `https://engineer.yadro.com/chip-design-school/`
   is the *School of Digital Circuit Synthesis* — a free two-semester program run by YADRO with MIET,
   MFTI and ITMO teaching staff, open to students, schoolchildren, engineers and teachers, delivered
   online or at partner university clusters. The club is evidently one of those clusters.

   So the `/club/join` primary CTA sends people to a **third party's program application**, not to a
   club membership form. The page copy must say that plainly, or a visitor who wanted to join the club
   on Saturday will be confused by an unrelated school signup. Proposed framing:

   > **1. Sign up for the Chip Design School** — the free YADRO/MIET course our sessions follow. ↗
   > **2. Join the Telegram chat** — where the room and the week's topic are posted. ↗
   > **3. Just come on Saturday.** No application is required to show up.

   🔴 Confirm this framing, or supply a club-specific form.

## 9. People

Roster scope, confirmed: **project authors + club officers/instructor.** No full membership list, no
alumni-of-everyone page.

- Every person credited on a project appears in `people.yaml`.
- Plus a small named leadership block: the faculty advisor and any student leads.
- Consent is recorded **by hand, in person**, by the leader.
- Removal is handled by the instructor / university staff / maintainer via a repository change.
- Legal compliance is the club leader's own responsibility, stated explicitly.

Schema consequence: **`name` is the only required field.** `role`, `photo`, `links`, `years` and
everything else are optional (`02-content-model.md` §2). A person can be credited with nothing but a
name, which is the minimum-consent case and must be the path of least resistance, not a special case.
